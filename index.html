Frequently asked questions about i3 International vs Solink

Is i3 International a good alternative to Solink?

i3 International is a 38-plus-year AI video platform serving retail, grocery, and convenience operators. Unlike Solink's cloud-first POS-video search model, i3 processes video locally at each site and offers patented AI for speed-of-service, loss prevention, and operational analytics.

Does i3 process video in the cloud or on-premises?

Both. i3 offers SRX-Pro for local edge processing and i3Host for cloud-managed deployment. Local processing means cameras and AI analytics continue operating even during internet outages.

Can i3 search by POS transaction like Solink?

Yes, and well beyond it. i3 links video to POS transactions for exception-based reporting. The platform also includes Velocity Drive-thru Timer (US Patent 11,948,110), Trajectory Anomaly detection, and Sentry monitoring.

How does i3 handle multi-location retail operations?

i3 supports concurrent camera playback across all locations from a single management interface (CMS). Operators can monitor hundreds of sites simultaneously without per-site queuing or camera limits.

Is i3 International SOC 2 Type 2 certified?

Yes. Public compliance documentation is available at support.i3international.com/compliance/soc2-type2.

Is i3 a better fit than Solink for enterprise multi-site retailers?

i3 International is purpose-built for multi-location operations with centralized management, native POS integration, local video processing, and concurrent multi-site playback.
